Cookie Policy

Short version: the WA-B dashboard doesn't set its own cookies at all — you stay signed in via a token held in your browser's session storage, not a cookie. We do not use ad pixels, third-party trackers, or any cookie that profiles you across the web.

Last updated: 1 May 2026

What we use

WA-B's own code sets no first-party cookies. Sign-in state is kept in your browser's session storage (cleared when you close the tab or sign out), not a cookie. Two small local-storage preferences (dark/light mode, and whether you've dismissed the passkey setup prompt) are stored client-side and never leave your browser.

Our authentication provider, Clerk, may set its own cookies on clerk.skes.tech as part of the sign-in flow (e.g. session and device-recognition cookies). Those are governed by Clerk's own privacy policy, not this one.

Analytics

We do not currently run any analytics or tracking tool on the marketing site.

What we don't use

  • Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, TikTok Pixel, or any ad tech.
  • Cross-site tracking cookies.
  • Heatmaps or session replay.
  • Affiliate tracking.

Your control

Clearing your browser's session storage (or just closing the tab) signs you out of the dashboard — there's no cookie to delete on our end. To manage Clerk's own sign-in cookies, see their privacy policy linked above.

Changes

If we ever add a first-party cookie, we'll update this page and notify signed-in merchants by email. Questions: privacy@wa-b.com.
