# Play Store listing copy — WA-B

Draft copy for the Google Play Console store listing. Character limits are
Play Store's hard limits — do not exceed them or the console will reject the
field.

## App name (max 30 characters)

```
WA-B — WhatsApp Shop Manager
```
(28 characters)

## Short description (max 80 characters)

```
Run your WhatsApp shop: orders, payments, inventory, and customers in one app.
```
(78 characters)

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
WA-B turns your WhatsApp Business number into a full storefront — and this
app is where you run it from.

Your customers already order from you on WhatsApp: browsing your catalog,
asking prices, sending "2x Jollof" and paying by Mobile Money, all inside a
chat they already have open. WA-B's bot handles that conversation
automatically, in English or Twi. This app is where you see it all, manage
it, and get paid.

WHAT YOU CAN DO FROM YOUR PHONE

• Orders — see every order the moment it comes in, mark it paid or
  delivered, and track status from "pending" to "delivered" without opening
  a laptop.
• Inbox — read the live conversation between the bot and each customer,
  step in yourself any time, and pause or resume the bot per conversation.
• Products & inventory — add products, update prices and stock, and get
  notified before you run out.
• Customers — see order history and repeat customers at a glance.
• Payments — Mobile Money and card payments via Paystack, reconciled
  automatically against each order.
• Promos & broadcasts — send a discount code or a re-engagement message to
  past customers.
• Analytics — today's orders, revenue, and trends without digging through a
  spreadsheet.
• Team access — bring on a manager or support agent with a role-scoped key
  that sees only what they need to.
• Works offline — orders and your dashboard are cached, so a shaky
  connection doesn't mean a blank screen.
• Secure sign-in — WhatsApp OTP or a passkey (fingerprint/face unlock), your
  choice.

BUILT FOR GHANAIAN SMES

Pricing, currency, and delivery timing are built around how businesses
actually run here — Ghana Cedis, Africa/Accra time, and MTN/Vodafone/
AirtelTigo Mobile Money all supported through a single checkout.

Requires a WA-B account (whatsapp.wa-b.com — sign up on the web first, then
link your business here). Not a public marketplace app — it's the private
management console for businesses already using WA-B.
```
(~1,950 characters — well under the 4,000 limit, room to expand later)

  re-exported at this size if it's already square/high-res enough — check
  before reusing.
- **Feature graphic**: 1024×500 PNG/JPEG, no alpha. Needs a design pass —
  nothing in the repo covers this size today.
- **Phone screenshots**: minimum 2, recommended 4–8, 16:9 or 9:16. Best
  captured live from the app (Orders, Inbox, Home/analytics, Products are
  the strongest screens to show).
- **Short/full description above** — ready to paste in as-is.
- **Privacy policy URL**: `https://skes.tech/wa-b/privacy.html` (already
  live).
- **Data safety form**: separate task — see the follow-up offer to draft
  those answers.
- **Content rating questionnaire**: answer in Play Console directly (a
  business/commerce app with no UGC or mature content should land in the
  lowest tier — "Everyone" — but only Play Console's own questionnaire
  produces the official rating).
